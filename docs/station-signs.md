# Permanent station QR signs

In the event operations desk, add and name the stations, then enable **Allow guest score reports**. Select **Keep QR invitations valid until revoked** and choose **Print station signs**. Select the stations you need and print the A4 preview, one sign per station. You can prepare signs before publishing the event. Guests can scan them once the event is published.

Each sign opens the guest reporting page focused on that station. Players can switch to **All stations** if their match moves. They can see the queue, start a queued match when their pool allows it, and submit a played score according to the event's approval policy. The station QR is an event invitation, so it does not restrict reporting to one station.

The QR invitation remains the same until an organiser selects **Revoke all guest passes**, disables guest reporting, or changes the invitation rotation setting. Revocation also invalidates active guest passes. A scan grants a one-hour pass; players can scan the same sign again when that pass expires. Unpublishing or closing the event prevents reporting even while a sign remains printed.

Rotating invitations remain the default for existing events. They change every 15 minutes and remain redeemable for at least an hour. They are unsuitable for signs printed before the event.
